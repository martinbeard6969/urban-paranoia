# The ladder

The book's deep structure: **the same shape at every scale.** Each level is a tenant of the one above, can't see past it, and passes the pressure down.

| Level | Above | Below | What flows up |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cosmic | The Freeholder | **The Tenants** | Rent: the harvest and the inventory |
| Planetary | The Tenants | **The Lodge** (the Table) | Obedience, the work, the list |
| Human power | The Seats | **Factors, then Stewards** | Instructions, silence |
| Everyday life | Larkspire, Coyle's, the platforms | **Nell, Danny, Jack** | Rent, labour, attention |

## Rules at every level

- **Nobody sees two rungs up.** Stewards don't know about the Seats, the Seats have never met the Tenants, and the Tenants have never seen the Freeholder.
- **Nobody says "I".** Every level speaks in the passive voice: "per instr.", "placement noted", "it has been agreed", *THERE IS TIME*.
- **Everyone is promised a place higher up.** Stewards get cash, Seats get the list, the Tenants hope to keep the lease.
- **Everyone fears an inspection.** Nell's 3:40 a.m. text from Larkspire and the Freeholder's visit are the same event at different scales.

## The reveal, one rung per stage

1. **ARG / Act 1:** a firm moved machines off the books (Coyle's).
2. **Act 1–2:** a secret circle is behind the firm (the Lodge).
3. **Act 2:** something non-human is behind the circle (the Tenants).
4. **Act 3:** even that is renting, and harvesting to pay rent ([the lease](the-tenants.md), [deep time](deep-time.md)).
5. **The film:** the Freeholder, and whether *it* is a tenant too.

## The key: sideways

- The ladder is **vertical**: power, rent, instructions and fear all run up and down.
- The only thing it can't model, inventory or harvest is **horizontal**: people on the same rung choosing each other.
- **The Wall** (ch 18): every thread runs sideways. At first that's the horror, because nothing points up. In the end it's the hope.
- **The ending** (ch 28–30): tenants on every rung refuse the inspection together.

**One line:** *Every ladder goes up. Nobody ever looked sideways.*
