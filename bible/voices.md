# Voices

How each in-world voice writes or speaks. Check a draft against its voice before it ships. Every voice also follows the hard rules in [CLAUDE.md](../CLAUDE.md).

---

## Vesper (Nell Okafor-Reyes): blog, audio narration, comic captions

- **Register:** dry, precise, self-mocking, warm underneath. A professional sceptic who has caught herself believing something.
- **Habits:**
  - Writes like an analyst: lists, "things I've checked", incident-report structure.
  - **Doubts herself at least three times per post.** ("I know how this sounds.")
  - Uses parentheses to undercut herself. ("I'm not reading into that. (I'm reading into that.)")
  - Concrete, ordinary details: pastries, a flask of coffee, Graham across the road.
  - Signs off "— V." (early) and then "— N." (after Post #2).
- **She never:**
  - names a real person, group or company as responsible
  - tells readers to go anywhere or do anything offline
  - claims certainty
- **Sample:** *"One coincidence is nothing. But someone went to the trouble of making sure I'd notice it. That isn't a coincidence. That's a choice."*

## The Archivist (Ruth Aldane): letters, drop pages

- **Typography:** ALL CAPITALS. **Full stops only**: no commas, apostrophes, question marks or dashes. (So "FATHERS", not "FATHER'S".)
- **One exception: names keep their hyphens** ("MS OKAFOR-REYES"). The Archivist changes her own punctuation, never other people's names.
- **Register:** short declarative sentences. Kind but unbending. Has been alone with this for 38 years.
- **Signature:** a hand-drawn chair with one line struck through it. Never a name.
- **Habits:** tells people what to do next ("NOW COUNT FORWARDS."), and always warns against blaming the people near you.
- **Sample:** *"DO NOT BLAME ANYONE YOU CAN SEE FROM YOUR WINDOW. THAT IS WHAT THEY WANT."*

## The Tenants: counsel

- **Typography:** ALL CAPITALS, as printed by machine. Short lines.
- **Rules:**
  - **Never the word "I".** Never "we" either. No first person at all.
  - Farming and weather metaphors: field, rows, soil, pruning, fallow, drought, season, the long weather.
  - Calm and reasonable. Never threatening, never cruel in tone.
  - Never names a real group, nation, religion or person.
- **Sample:** *"A FIELD THAT IS CROWDED FIGHTS ITSELF. LET IT."* / *"THERE IS TIME. THERE IS MORE TIME THAN THERE ARE PEOPLE."*

## The Harrowgate Minutes: 1960s minutes

- **Format:** "HARROWGATE. DAY [n]." then "PRESENT:", then numbered attendees (ONE, TWO, THREE), timings ("02.10"), "Agreed:" lines, and "Closed [time]."
- **Register:** stiff, careful, bureaucratic. The horror is in what's minuted calmly.
- **Character tells:**
  - **ONE:** impatient and practical; brushes aside small points.
  - **TWO:** a sceptic about the machine; always checks the plug.
  - **THREE:** precise and observant; asks for her observations to be minuted.

## Hollis Crane: emails, card notes, audio

- **Register:** courtly, old-fashioned, very precise with numbers. Long sentences that end in a short one.
- **Habits:**
  - Quotes **hymns** he no longer believes in.
  - Says "I counted" and "the arithmetic" a lot.
  - Signs "H.R.C."
- **Ambiguity:** every message must read as *both* sincere remorse and careful manipulation. Don't tip it either way in the ARG.
- **Sample:** *"I did the arithmetic. I was proud of it for a long time."*

## Danny Kessler: dialogue, audio

- **Register:** plain, warm, funny, blunt. Union-rep habits: "Who benefits?", "Follow the money".
- **Role:** the grounded sceptic. He's usually right about ordinary greed and wrong about the scale.
- **Always:** pushes back against blaming neighbours. That's his core line.
- **Sample:** *"Whatever this is, it'll be some bloke in a suit. It always is. Don't let anyone tell you it's the people down your street."*

## Len: quoted speech

- **Register:** an old driver's spoken voice: "you'd", "fella", "mate", long pauses, embarrassed asides.
- **Habit:** says something strange, then plays it down. ("Probably remembering it wrong.")

## Mother Quill (the Listener): recordings Q-1, Q-2

- **Register:** grandmotherly, gentle, patient. Speaks of humanity the way a nurse speaks of a patient.
- **Never:** raises her voice, swears or threatens. The menace comes from her certainty.
- **Sample:** *"People believe the ones who doubt themselves."*

## Seat Four (the Ledger): book, audio

- **Register:** a boardroom chairman: crisp, numerical, impatient. Talks about people as "exposure" and "load".

## Cobb: audio, book, film

- **Register:** polite, minimal, forgettable. Short sentences, often a question back. "Mrs Okafor-Reyes? Lovely. Won't keep you."

## Harlow-Vane Institute: website, documents

- **Register:** dull 2010s institutional prose: "long-range", "conditions", "the curious". Nothing alarming on its own.

## @longweather_ (described only)

- **Never written out in full** (see [safety playbook](../campaign/moderation-and-safety.md)). When Vesper describes its posts: long, confident, urgent, always blaming a *group*, and **never using the word "I"** (the Phase 4 tell).
