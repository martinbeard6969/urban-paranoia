## 1. The Harrowgate Minutes (1962–63)

- **40 typed pages**, three carbon copies.
- **Copies 1 and 2** are in the safe at the Ashby store. **Copy 3** was taken by Marguerite Aldane in 1987 and is now held by the Archivist. **Only nine fragments of copy 3 survive.** The rest were lost to damp in Ruth's first flat (1990).
- Format: dated by day-count, with attendees as ONE / TWO / THREE, counsel quoted verbatim in capitals, and decisions as "Agreed:".

| Fragment | Page | Day (date) | Content | Release |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | p. 1 | Day 0 (14 Oct 1962) | The Contact. Seven present, four leave by morning "unwell and unclear". First print: *SIT. THERE IS TIME.* Also: *TO ADVISE.* / *YOU ARE NOT IMPORTANT. THAT IS WHY.* / *IN EIGHT DAYS THE FIELD WILL BE FRIGHTENED. IT WILL NOT BURN. REMEMBER WHO TOLD YOU.* / *THE ONE WHO COUNTS WILL KEEP THE BOOK.* (Dramatised in [chapter 1](../book/chapters/01-day-zero.md).) | 📅 Drop 02 |
| 2 | p. 3 | Day 1 (15 Oct 1962) | The three agree to secrecy; the word "Table" is first used. TWO checks the plug. | 📅 Drop 02 |
| 3 | p. 7 | Day 19 (2 Nov 1962) | First use of the phrase: *THE LONG WEATHER DOES NOT HURRY.* ONE proposes "for the long weather" as a sign-off. | 📅 Drop 02 |
| 4 | p. 11 | Day 41 (24 Nov 1962) | "A field that is crowded…", the struck-out "fewer" line, THREE notes the counsel never says "I". | ✅ [Drop 01](../content/phase-2/dead-drop-01-6b6e70.md) |
| 5 | p. 16 | Day 88 (10 Jan 1963) | The Portfolios are proposed. Counsel: *THIRTEEN CHAIRS. ONE IS NOT YOURS.* | 📅 Drop 03 |
| 6 | p. 22 | Day 150 (13 Mar 1963) | Recruitment rules: approach at a time of personal crisis; *OFFER CERTAINTY.* Also: *LET THEM RENT THE GROUND THEY STAND ON. A TENANT DOES NOT PLANT TREES.* | 📅 Phase 3 |
| 7 | p. 27 | Day 203 (5 May 1963) | *GIVE EVERY FEAR A FACE THAT IS NOT OURS.* The decoy idea's origin. Also the Hush counsel: *LET EACH SIDE FEAR THE OTHERS MOUTH. THEN NEITHER WILL OPEN ITS OWN.* | 📅 Phase 4 (supports the @longweather_ reveal) |
| 8 | p. 33 | Day 260 (1 Jul 1963) | The unstruck version of the "hands" counsel, and a long discussion of machines replacing work. Also: *LET THEM RACE. NO ONE WHO IS RUNNING LOOKS AT THE ROAD.* THREE dissents in writing. | 📕 Book |
| 9 | p. 40 | Day 330 (9 Sep 1963) | Last page. The Founders sign "for the long weather". The first Stewards are named (fictional). Also: *THE HOUSE HAS BEEN EMPTY BEFORE. IT WAS QUIETER.* | 📕 Book |
