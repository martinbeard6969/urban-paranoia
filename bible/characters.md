# Characters

All characters are fictional. None is based on a real, identifiable person.

---

## Protagonists

### Vesper (real name: Nell Okafor-Reyes)
- **Role:** anonymous blogger who starts the ARG; the audio series narrator; the novel's point-of-view character.
- **Age:** 38.
- **Background:** freight logistics analyst, laid off when an AI routing system replaced her team. Good with spreadsheets, bad at sleep.
- **What starts it:** while archiving old company files, she finds a 1971 shipping manifest stamped "for the long weather".
- **Voice:** dry, precise, self-mocking. Doubts herself in nearly every post. *"I know how this sounds. I'm writing it down anyway."*
- **Arc:** from pattern-spotter to someone who understands that the patterns are designed to make her blame the wrong people. Her climax is choosing *connection over suspicion*.
- **Secret:** her late father was a Steward without knowing it.
- **Missing knock (D14):** her post *"Three streets, one pattern?"* is shared by @longweather_ (ch 17). In ch 18 and 20 she says on the page that she believed it for nine days, and retracts. Complicit: she lent the rumour her credibility.

### Dr. Hollis Crane
- **Role:** mentor, or manipulator.
- **Age:** 81.
- **Background:** retired development economist. Spent the 1970s at the fictional **Harlow-Vane Institute for Systems Studies**.
- **Function:** gives Vesper context: real economic history, described accurately, read through a terrifying lens.
- **Ambiguity:** he may be a Gardener defector, or the Lodge's chosen handler for her. The novel keeps both possible until Act 3. *Writers' room:* he is both, in turn: handler first, then defector (canon decisions D6).
- **Tic:** quotes hymns he no longer believes in.
- **Harlow-Vane record (card 0144):** born 1945; joined 2 Apr 1973; Senior Fellow and **H.H. liaison** from 1979 (sat in on contact sessions "with T."); built **Model 7** (fertility response to precarity), "commended" 1984, "reservations noted" 1991; withdrew his 2006 paper; "Weather status: under review" since 2009. Honorary Archivist of the Harlow-Vane holding site since 2010, which is how he learns that card 0144 has been opened.
- **Haunted by** Marguerite's 1987 question: "*which* people".
- **Missing knock (D14):** knows a detail of death 1 that Nell hadn't told him (ch 14). Explained in ch 25: a Factor briefed him. Complicit, over the long term: he built the measure.

### The Archivist
- **Role:** unseen source who leaves documents for Vesper (in dead drops, PO boxes, and files hidden on sites).
- **Truth (book reveal):** **Ruth Aldane**, daughter of the ex-Seat Seven who walked out in 1987. She has spent 38 years (since 1988) assembling her mother's papers. In book Act 3 Crane tells her Marguerite's death was a genuine accident that the Lodge let people believe was murder (canon decisions D7).
- **Voice:** messages in typed caps, no punctuation except full stops. Signs off with a drawing of a thirteenth chair crossed out.

### Danny Kessler
- **Role:** Vesper's friend and sceptic; a former union organiser who now drives for a delivery app.
- **Function:** keeps the story grounded. He argues the "plan" is just ordinary greed and policy, and he is often right. He also voices the cost of the scapegoating the Lodge stirs up, because his neighbourhood is torn by it.
- **Arc:** the novel's emotional heart. He helps rebuild a small, mixed community network that the Lodge cannot divide.
- **History with the Okafors:** a family friend since Nell was nine. He argued with Emmanuel on Sunday afternoons about cricket and pensions. He was the union rep across the table at Coyle's first cuts. After Emmanuel's death in 2019 he made Nell his responsibility, without saying so. (Ch 5; he may know something about Emmanuel's 1987 refusal, to use in ch 19.)
- **Details:** **57** (in 2026); a **granddaughter** at the local school, in the same class as the child of the family whose window was bricked (ch 5); works **nights at a warehouse** three times a week as a second job, for the same company he delivers for. Delivers for an app, rated 4.9 ("the most stressful number of my life"). Shows care through action: chips, "ring me when you've eaten".
- **Missing knock (D14):** he was at all three doors the week before (ch 13, 16, 17). Explained in ch 18: the app's ninety-second door timer (ch 5); he left the parcels. Complicit a little: the system punished knocking, and he obeyed it.

## Antagonists

### The Listener ("Mother Quill")
- **Role:** the only current Lodge member who receives Tenant counsel directly.
- **Age:** 96 (born 1930).
- **Background:** a New Zealander. In 1966 she was a records clerk at her country's national weather service, which the Table found grimly funny. Seated 1966; Listener since 1976.
- **Now:** a widow with a family foundation, three generations of heirs, and a farm she never leaves. Her grandchildren think she was "an early investor in computers".
- **Presence:** almost never seen. Heard on two leaked recordings. Calm, kind-sounding, terrifying.
- **Belief:** sincerely thinks she is saving humanity from itself.

### Seat Four ("The Ledger")
- **Role:** the active, hands-on antagonist. Holds the money-and-prices Portfolio.
- **Background:** a Canadian, about 80. In 1979 he was a mid-level insurance regulator. Seated the same year, as the chair's second holder: he replaced one of the nine seated by 1971.
- **Cover identity:** chair of the fictional **Larkspire Mutual Re** reinsurance group, which holds the Table's stakes in Tollgate and the AI labs.
- **Family:** two children on the boards of the family foundation; one grandchild works at Lanternwork and has no idea.
- **Method:** works through Stewards. Sends **Cobb** after Vesper.
- **Belief:** an Accountant. Thinks the plan is simply rational.

### Cobb
- **Role:** fixer, Lodge Steward.
- **Function:** physical threat in the audio series and the film. Polite, forgettable, always slightly too close.
- **Before he speaks (Act One pacing):** three unnamed sightings: at the next table in Len's café (ch 3), at the wheel of the bird-logo van (ch 5), walking a dog past Nell's window (ch 6). Plain enough not to stick; in ch 10 he lets her know it was him each time.
- **Twist:** Cobb doesn't know what the Lodge is either. He is a victim of the design too.
- **Missing knock (D14):** seen on death 3's street the day before (ch 17). Explained in ch 20: as "a man from the letting agent", he was the only one who knocked. Not complicit.
