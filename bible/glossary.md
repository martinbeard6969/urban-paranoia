# Glossary

In-world terms, with who uses them. **(L)** Lodge internal · **(HV)** Harlow-Vane · **(C)** Coyle's · **(V)** Vesper and the public

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Accountants** (L) | The Lodge faction that sees the plan as rational stewardship. Seat Four leads it. |
| **Apparatus, the** (L) | Whatever machine the counsel comes through: the teletype, the Exchange, fax, now servers. |
| **Ashby store** (L) | The bonded warehouse holding the teletype, the Exchange and copies 1–2 of the Minutes. |
| **Carrying-capacity & Social Stability** (HV) | The real meaning of **C.S.S.** Public cover: "Centre for Systems Studies". |
| **Chair, the empty / thirteenth** (L) | Seat Thirteen, the Tenants' place at the Table. Never sat in. |
| **Counters, the** (deep time) | The clerks the Tenants have spoken to across every era: scribes, census-takers, actuaries. The Lodge is the latest. |
| **Counsel** (L) | A message from the Tenants. Never "orders" or "instructions". |
| **Clearfell** (V) | A fictional left-coded online group for "the people being priced out". Its recruiters are secretly Lodge Stewards. Forestry term for felling every tree in an area. |
| **Crossed chair** (V) | The Archivist's signature: the Lodge's chair, refused. |
| **D. [n] / Day [n]** (L, C) | Lodge dating: days since 14 Oct 1962 (Day 0). |
| **Decoy, Decoy Doctrine** (L) | Giving every hardship a human scapegoat so that no one looks at the Lodge. Formalised in 2014 from Fragment 7's counsel. |
| **Cull, the** | Book and film only. The clearing of the field at the end of the lease. Method never shown (canon decision D11). |
| **Exchange, the** (L) | The 1983–94 telephone-exchange cabinet that captured counsel as crosstalk. "Hummed with the power off." |
| **Fallow** (L) | A deliberate period of economic stagnation. |
| **Fewer** (L) | Banned word, Day 41. It means population, not jobs. |
| **Freeholder, the** | What the Tenants lease Earth from. Never seen. Sends inspections. |
| **Founders** (L) | ONE, TWO and THREE, the original contactees. All are now dead. |
| **Frostline** (V) | A fictional right-coded online group defending "the people being replaced". Its recruiters are secretly Lodge Stewards, in the same office as Clearfell's. The depth below which the ground freezes. |
| **Gardeners** (L) | The faction that has started to doubt what "pruning" means. Marguerite was the first. |
| **Hush, the** (L) | The strand of the Decoy Doctrine that works both sides of every speech fight until people choose silence. Run by the Signal and Faith Seats. See [modern pressures §4](modern-pressures.md). |
| **Hands** (L) | Workers, labour. "When the hands are no longer needed…" |
| **Harrowgate grey** (L) | `#6B6E70`, the Lodge's colour. |
| **Harvest, the** | What the Tenants take as tenant farmers: humanity's technologies, resources and intelligence, and humans themselves. |
| **H.H.** (C, HV) | Harrowgate House. |
| **House, the** (HV) | Harlow-Vane staff slang for the Lodge, by people who half knew. |
| **Inspection, the** | The Freeholder's visit. A loud, crowded household fails it. At human scale: Larkspire's "routine inspection". |
| **Inventory, the** | Part of the rent: a complete record of the field and everything in it. The AI race is writing it. |
| **Lease, the** | The Tenants' tenancy of Earth. See [the-tenants.md](the-tenants.md). |
| **Listener, the** (L) | The one member who receives counsel directly. Currently Mother Quill. |
| **Listening Room** (L) | The teletype room at Harrowgate House. |
| **Long weather, the** (L) | The Tenants' timescale: change too slow to notice. As a sign-off ("for the long weather"), it means *we are patient*. |
| **Model 7** (HV) | Crane's model of how falling birth rates respond to economic insecurity. |
| **Per instr.** (C) | "Per instruction". Coyle's code for an off-the-books Lodge job. |
| **Portfolio** (L) | A Seat's area of slow influence: Faith, Assembly, Signal, Ledger, Engine, Border, Hearth, and so on. |
| **Plant trees** (L) | Put down roots: own, stay, build. What the "rent the ground" counsel aims to prevent. |
| **Pressure tide** (L) | Several small stresses timed to land together. |
| **Pruning / thinning** (L) | Population falling on its own through precarity, isolation and despair. Never a visible act. |
| **Seat** (L) | A numbered chair at the Table. It passes to the next person seated. |
| **Seated** (L) | Admitted to the Table. |
| **Race, the** (L) | The AI race, as the Engine Seat sees it: "No one who is running looks at the road." |
| **Steward** (L) | A helper who does tasks without knowing the whole design. |
| **Taken, the** | The "brightest and most attractive" (to the harvest), taken before the cull. The Lodge's families were promised places. |
| **Table, the** (L) | What the Lodge calls itself. "The Lodge" is the outsiders' name. |
| **Tenants, the** / **T.** (L) | The non-human intelligence. Lodge paperwork writes only "T." |
| **Three tones** (L, V) | The G–E♭–G chime that ends a session. |
| **Tenant farmers** | What the Tenants are: they develop the field, keep a share, pay rent. |
| **Twelve by twelve / 144** (V) | The grid that shows up in machine errors, and the 144 lb weights. |
| **Walkout, the** (L) | Marguerite Aldane leaving the Table on 16 Apr 1987 (Day 8,950). |
| **Weather (clearance)** (L, HV) | Knowing about the Tenants. "Weather ✓" means told; "Weather ✗" means used but not told. |
