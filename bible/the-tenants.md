# The Tenants

> "They do not want the house. They want the people in it to stop moving the furniture."
> (Vesper, Post #19)

## Summary

The Tenants are a non-human intelligence. Their nature is **never confirmed on the page**, and that ambiguity is a core rule of the series. They have no ships, no invasion and no bodies anyone has seen. They influence humanity entirely through **counsel** given to a handful of people, and through patience measured in generations.

## What the audience is allowed to know

| Layer | Revealed in | What's learned |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | ARG Phase 1–2 | "Something" advised a group in 1962. Could be a hoax, a cult, a madness. |
| 2 | ARG Phase 3 / audio S1 | Contact happened through machines (teletype, later telephone exchanges, later data centres). |
| 3 | Book, Act 2 | The Tenants experience time differently. They plan in centuries. |
| 4 | Book, Act 3 | Their goal: a **smaller, stiller** humanity. They are **tenant farmers**: the harvest, the inventory, the Taken, the cull, and the earlier Tables (see "The lease" and [deep time](deep-time.md)). |
| 5 | Film / later | **The Freeholder.** Even the Tenants pay rent, and an inspection is coming (see [the ladder](the-ladder.md)). |

## How they communicate

- **Through infrastructure, never in person.** Teletype (1962), telephone exchange crosstalk (1970s–80s), fax noise (1990s), and now **anomalous output in automated systems**.
- **In counsel, not commands.** Their messages are gentle, reasonable and plausible. That is what makes them frightening.
- **The "Long Weather" register.** They talk about societies the way a farmer talks about weather and soil: *drought, pruning, fallow seasons, thinning the rows*.

### Sample counsel (canon fragments from the Harrowgate Minutes)
- *"A field that is crowded fights itself. Let it."*
- *"Do not take their gods. Let the gods grow quiet."*
- *"When the hands are no longer needed, the hands will be fewer."*
- *"Give every fear a face that is not ours."*

## The plan, as the Tenants frame it

1. **Unmoor**: loosen shared anchors such as faith, party and place.
2. **Unbind**: dissolve collective leverage (unions, guilds, community institutions).
3. **Unsettle**: keep populations under constant low-level economic strain.
4. **Divide**: make sure every hardship has a human scapegoat.
5. **Replace**: let machines make mass labour unnecessary.
6. **Thin**: let numbers fall on their own, through declining birth rates, despair and attrition. **Never through any visible act.**

**Writing rule:** the "thinning" is always slow, impersonal and deniable. Never depict it as targeting any real-world group, and never suggest real public-health measures, medicines or vaccines are part of it. **The cull** (below) exists **only in the book and film**, and its method is **never shown** (canon decision D11).

## The lease (writers' room canon; revealed in Act 3 and the film)

**The arrangement.** The Tenants don't own Earth. They **lease** it from **the Freeholder**, something vast and never seen, and they work it as **tenant farmers**:
- **The field** is Earth. **The crop** is humanity: what we make (technologies, resources, intelligence) and **what we are** (minds, habits, voices).
- **The farmhands** are the Lodge, paid in kind, which is why they got rich.
- **The rent** has two parts: **a share of the harvest**, and **the inventory**, a complete, ordered record of the field and everything in it.
- **Developers, not just overseers:** since 1962 the counsel has steered humanity towards containers, computers, networks, phones and AI, because **that's the crop**. Every Lodge investment (Pellucid, Tollgate, Halvard, Nimfold, Lanternwork) is the Tenants developing the farm.
- **The AI race is the rent collection.** Devices capture, the cloud stores, and models compress us into something that can be handed over. *LET THEM RACE*: the faster they race, the sooner the inventory is complete.

**Keeping the field alive.** A farmer who exhausts the soil early gets nothing. So the Tenants keep Earth **sustainable until the harvest**: frightened but never burned (*IT WILL NOT BURN*, 1962), stretched but never broken.

**The Taken.** Before the end of the lease, the **brightest and most attractive** are taken and the rest are left to the thinning.
- **"Attractive" means attractive to the harvest**: whoever draws the most attention, data and influence. It never means physical appearance, and the selection is **never** by race, ethnicity, religion, nationality, gender, disability or any real group (canon decision D12).
- **What "taken" means is never confirmed:** copied whole into the inventory, or carried off when the lease ends.
- **The Lodge's real motive:** its members were promised their families would be Taken. The list, not the money, is why nobodies served for sixty years.
- **The twist:** the "best" are simply **the easiest to harvest**, the most legible and most fully captured. To the Tenants, being Taken means being finished. The people they *can't* inventory are ordinary, stubborn people who choose each other.

**The cull (book and film only).** At the end of the lease, the Tenants mean to **clear the field**: keep the harvest and reduce the occupants to whatever makes the house easy to hand back. "Fewer" was always a **date**. Crane's Model 7 was forecasting it. **Its method is never shown and never linked to any real-world mechanism** (canon decision D11).

**Why they're afraid.** They've been evicted before. They're tenants too, and an **inspection** is coming. A loud, crowded, ungovernable household fails.

**What *INTERESTING* means (ch 30).** The Tenants see something that isn't on any inventory and can't be harvested: tenants on every rung refusing an inspection together. Either it threatens the lease, or it's the first thing on this farm worth keeping. The cull is **postponed, not cancelled**.

**How far back it goes:** see [deep time](deep-time.md). **How it's structured:** see [the ladder](the-ladder.md).

## Why? (open theories, for fan speculation)

Seed these in the ARG and let the community argue:
- **The Gardener theory:** humanity is a crop, and the Tenants are managing yield. *(Closest to the truth. Let fans get there.)*
- **The Quarantine theory:** a large, loud humanity would attract something worse.
- **The Inheritance theory:** they are preparing Earth for someone else.
- **The Mirror theory:** there are no Tenants. The Lodge invented them so they would never have to own their choices. (*The story should keep this one possible to the very end.*)

## Tells in the world (ARG puzzle hooks)

- A **three-tone chime** (G–E♭–G) heard in old recordings of the contact.
- Text from the Tenants **never uses the word "I"**.
- Errors in automated systems that form **12×12 grids**.
