### What would stop you locking a chapter

There is **no locked status, and nothing technical prevents an edit**. These processes would all change a chapter you had rewritten:

| Process | Where | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Fixes applied without asking when I agree | `CLAUDE.md`, creative critic section, step 3 | **Main risk.** Your prose could be edited with no question asked |
| Chapter redrafts and polish passes | Practice in this project; outline "rewrite notes" | Overwrites prose wholesale |
| Style ceilings | `CLAUDE.md` Voice & style; fixes v1.2, v1.4 | Your simile or sting counts would invite edits |
| Continuity fixes from other chapters | Working practice (e.g. ch 6's "chips" fix) | A later change can trigger an edit to yours |
| Canon and rule corrections | Hard rules; conventions | Must still be able to happen, so these need a route to you, not silence |
| Commit habits | All commits carry my trailer under your git user | Your rewrite would look the same as my draft |

**Generated files aren't a risk:** PDFs, EPUB and the NotebookLM pack only copy chapter text.

**Proposed fix, not yet made:**
- Add frontmatter to each chapter: `text-status: locked` and `text-by: Martin`, or a percentage or passage note.
- Add to `CLAUDE.md`: I never edit a locked chapter. Fixes, polish and continuity changes touching it go to you as suggestions, even if I agree with them. Hard-rule problems get flagged immediately.
- Commit your rewrites yourself, with no Claude trailer and a message saying so, to record a clear date and author.
- Keep your own drafts, dated, outside the repo as well.
- Optional: a git pre-commit check that refuses changes to locked chapters without an override.

## 6. Decisions needed from you

1. **How much will you write yourself?** Which chapters, if any, and what target? This drives the credit line and the locking plan.
2. **Domain structure. My recommendation is B.**
   - **A:** in-world sites as subdomains of `urbanparanoia.com`. Cheapest and simple, and makes the trail obvious, but the title appears in every puzzle URL, and the "is this real?" effect suffers.
   - **B:** `urbanparanoia.com` as the out-of-world hub (`/about`, sign-up, book), with separate in-world domains for Harrowgate and Harlow-Vane, each footer-linked to the hub. It keeps the title rule, at a small extra yearly cost.
   - **C:** amend the title rule to allow A.
3. **Who is "the publisher"?** You under your own name, a company, or a future publisher? This settles WHOIS, the data controller, the escalation contact and who signs assignments.
4. **The credit line.** Adopt the working wording for now? It reads "Created and directed by Martin Defries. Drafted and edited with AI under the author's direction." Should "copyright" stay in the EPUB meanwhile? **I recommend removing it until the solicitor has advised.**
5. **Solicitor.** Who, when, and with what budget? I'd do this before launch and before any agent submission.
6. **Public stance on AI.** Disclose only where required or asked, or state it plainly on `/about`? I recommend `/about`, since this audience investigates.
7. **Route to market.** Direct-first, agent-first or both? KDP Select depends on this.
8. **Trade mark.** Commission a clearance search now? Which classes do you genuinely intend to use?
9. **The cover.** How was it made, and with what tool? I can't tell from the repo. Also: keep the St Paul's-like dome?
10. **Author site.** Do you want `martindefries.com` at all? Rechecking it first is cheap.
11. **HG-68 cast.** Actors or a friend? Either way, paperwork comes first.
12. **Reader panel.** Were they real people? If so, was anything agreed about their suggestions?
13. **Changes from this report.** Which should I make: checklist additions, the placeholder replacement, the locking rule, or the EPUB line? Nothing happens until you say.
