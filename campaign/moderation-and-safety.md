# Moderation & safety playbook

The campaign deliberately blurs the line between fiction and reality, and it deals with themes (economic insecurity, distrust of elites, hidden plans) that real conspiracy movements use. This playbook keeps that blur **inside the story**. Everyone who posts in character must read it.

## Principles

1. **The ambiguity is an atmosphere, not a trap.** Anyone who asks sincerely whether this is real gets a truthful path to the answer.
2. **People come before immersion.** When immersion conflicts with someone's wellbeing or safety, break character.
3. **The story condemns scapegoating, so moderation must too.** We never let our channels host real-world blame of real groups, even "in character".
4. **Nothing leaves the screen.** No real-world locations, meet-ups, deliveries or confrontations unless they're organised, ticketed events that are openly part of the campaign.

## Comment moderation (Vesper's blog and campaign channels)

| Comment type | Action |
|---|---|
| Theories, puzzle-solving, speculation about **fictional** entities | Allow. Encourage. |
| Scepticism, "this is an ARG", "this is marketing" | Allow. Don't delete or argue. Vesper can reply lightly in character ("D. says the same"). |
| Blaming a **real** ethnic, religious, national or migrant group, or a real political movement | **Delete.** Don't reply in character with arguments. Repeat offenders are banned. Log it. |
| Naming **real people** as Lodge members or Stewards | **Delete.** Log it. |
| Real addresses, phone numbers, or anyone's personal details (including guesses at the "real" Vesper or Crane) | **Delete at once.** Ban. Escalate if it looks like doxxing. |
| Plans to visit, watch or confront real places or people | **Delete.** Post an out-of-character note (see below). Escalate. |
| Health, financial or safety claims (e.g. "the plan is in the vaccines", "pull your money out") | **Delete.** Out-of-character note if it's spreading. |
| Distress, or apparent belief that the person is personally being watched or targeted | **Break character privately** (see below). |
| Abuse, harassment, threats | Delete, ban, escalate. |

### Out-of-character note (public)
Post it from a clearly labelled account (e.g. "Urban Paranoia team"), never from Vesper:

> Quick note from the team behind this story: *Urban Paranoia* is a work of fiction. Please don't go looking for any real places or people mentioned in the comments. Everything in this story lives online. [About the project](/about)

## Book-only themes in player spaces

The cull, the Taken, deep time and the rent strike belong to the book and film. If players bring them into the game:

| Player content | Action |
|---|---|
| Linking "the cull" or "the thinning" to real vaccines, medicine, food, technology, companies or events | **Delete.** Post the out-of-character note. Escalate if it spreads. |
| Claiming real people or groups are "Taken", "chosen" or "being thinned" | **Delete.** Ban repeat offenders. |
| Crediting real ancient monuments or cultures to aliens ("ancient aliens") | **Delete.** Brief out-of-character note: in this story, humans built everything. |
| Linking the story to real riots or unrest, or calling for real-world violence or confrontation | **Delete at once.** Escalate. |
| Discussing the fictional Larkspire rent strike as story | Allow. |
| Asking about real tenants' rights | Allow, and point to official tenant-advice services. Never give legal advice in character. |

## Breaking character with an individual

**Triggers:** distress, fear for their own safety, signs of paranoia that seem to go beyond enjoying the game, signs they're a minor, or a direct and sincere "please tell me if this is real".

**How:** a private message from the team account, not from a character:

> Hi, this is the team behind *Urban Paranoia*, the story Vesper's blog is part of. It's fiction: Vesper, the Lodge and the Tenants are all invented. We wanted to make sure you knew that, and we're glad you're enjoying it. If any of it has been weighing on you, it's completely fine to step away. [About the project](/about)

Add local support resources if the person seems to be in real distress. Don't push them to keep playing.

## @longweather_ (special handling)

@longweather_ is the antagonist account that spreads decoy blame. **It's the most dangerous asset in the campaign**, because done badly, it *is* real-world scapegoating.

- **Default: it exists only in the story.** It's described in Vesper's posts and seen as deleted or summarised comments on her blog. We never write its blame content in full, and it never names a real group.
- **It must not be created as a real account on any social platform** without:
  - a written plan for what it posts, reviewed against the hard rules
  - legal and platform-policy sign-off
  - its content being limited to *fictional* targets only (e.g. it blames "the Harlow-Vane people" or "the Ashby lot", never real groups)
- If real users start quoting or copying the account's style to blame real groups, handle it as a scapegoating comment: delete, don't engage, and log it. Bring the Phase 4 reveal forward if it becomes a pattern.

## In-world inboxes (e.g. Crane's `archive@`)

- Only open one if someone is staffing it, with replies within 48 hours.
- Every reply follows the rules in this playbook and the hard rules. Vulnerable or distressed correspondents get the break-character message.
- Every reply's signature includes a small "a story by…" line linking to `/about`.
- Never ask correspondents for personal details. Never send anything physical to a player's address.

## Press and enquiries

- If a journalist asks whether this is real, **confirm it's a fiction campaign for the book**, off the record if necessary. Don't lie to the press.
- If a regulator, platform or law-enforcement body asks, disclose fully and at once.

## Escalation

| Severity | Examples | Who | Timing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Low | Repeated off-topic or scapegoating comments | Moderator | Same day |
| Medium | Doxxing attempts, confrontation plans, distressed players | Moderator → named publisher contact | Within 2 hours |
| High | Threats, real-world incidents, press reporting it as real | Publisher contact → legal | Immediately. Pause new content until resolved. |

## Incident log

Keep a simple log (date, channel, what happened, action taken). Review it weekly during the campaign and before each phase starts.
