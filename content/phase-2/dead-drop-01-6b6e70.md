# Dead Drop 01: `/6b6e70`

## Page design (for build)

- **Background:** solid Harrowgate grey `#6B6E70`, edge to edge. No header, navigation or logo.
- **Text:** off-white `#ECEAE4` in a monospaced typewriter face (e.g. *Special Elite* or *Courier Prime* from Google Fonts). Left-aligned, in a narrow column of about 60 characters, with generous space.
- **Load behaviour:** the page opens blank and grey. After about 2 seconds the first line types itself out at roughly 40 characters a second. Everything else fades in once it finishes. Visitors who prefer reduced motion see all the text at once.
- **Mobile:** single column, 16px side gutters, scans open full-screen on tap.
- **Nothing else:** no analytics banners, pop-ups or sign-ups. The page should feel like a room someone left unlocked on purpose.

### ITEM ONE. THE HARROWGATE MINUTES. FRAGMENT FOUR.

*[Scan: one typed page, a carbon copy faded to the blue of old veins. Top right: "p. 11 of 40". Top left: "COPY 3 OF 3. NOT TO LEAVE THE HOUSE." One line is struck through by hand in pencil.]*

**Transcription (beneath the scan):**

> HARROWGATE. DAY 41.
> PRESENT: ONE, TWO, THREE.
>
> The apparatus commenced at 02.10 and ran until 02.55. Paper consumed: approx. 6 ft. As on previous occasions, the machine was not connected to any line. TWO examined the plug himself and asks that this be minuted.
>
> Counsel received (verbatim; spacing as printed):
>
> — A FIELD THAT IS CROWDED FIGHTS ITSELF. LET IT.
> — DO NOT TAKE THEIR GODS. LET THE GODS GROW QUIET.
> — THERE IS TIME. THERE IS MORE TIME THAN THERE ARE PEOPLE.
> — ~~WHEN THE HANDS ARE NO LONGER NEEDED THE HANDS WILL BE FEWER.~~
>
> ONE enquired whether "fewer" was meant in the sense of employment or of population. The apparatus did not reply. It sounded the three tones (ref. HG/A/02) and stopped.
>
> THREE observed that the counsel never employs the first person. ONE observed that this was "not the most pressing point". THREE asks that her observation be minuted regardless.
>
> Agreed: to approach a further four persons for seating before Day 400.
> Agreed: the word "fewer" will not be used outside this room.
>
> Closed 03.20.

*In the margin beside the struck line, in a different hand and a later pencil:*

> *They crossed it out so they'd never have to read it again. You can still read it. That's the trouble with pencil. — M.*

### ITEM TWO. A NOTE. 1987.

*[Scan: a sheet torn from a spiral notebook, blue biro, two coffee rings overlapping like a Venn diagram of the same bad night. In the corner, "D. 8,9—", with the last digits torn away.]*

**Transcription:**

> I asked them tonight what "thinning" means, and nobody would look at me.
>
> Four said it's gardening. That a gardener isn't cruel. That if you stop watering a row it comes out by itself: no work, no faith, no neighbour you trust, and people simply stop *having* people. No one is hurt. Everyone is just fewer.
>
> I said a gardener still chooses the rows.
>
> The Listener said I'd been at the table too long to be squeamish. She said it kindly. That's the worst of it. They're all so *kind*.
>
> I'm standing up. I've been standing up in my head for three years. Tonight my legs will have to catch up.
>
> H.C. knew. He did the arithmetic at Harlow-Vane. I've seen his tables. Ask H.C. what C.S.S. was counting.
>
> If anyone ever reads this: it was never the people they told you to be angry at. It was us. Thirteen chairs. Never more.
>
> — M.
