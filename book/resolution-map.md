### Book 2 setting (Martin, 28 Oct 2026; reference only)

- **When:** about ten years after Book 1, so around 2036.
- **Where:** America. Civil war has broken out on the East and West coasts. Vesper (Nell) has moved to **Washington, DC**, the capital, between the two fronts (Martin, 28 Oct 2026).
- **Guardrails (hard rules 1, 2, 4, 5 and 9):**
  - The war is **invented**. Its sides, leaders, militias and flashpoints are fictional composites, never mapped onto real US parties, movements, states' politics or real people.
  - The twist holds: the Lodge feeds **every side at once**, and no real group is ever the war's cause or instrument.
  - No real US event (an election, attack, riot or disaster) is retold or blamed on the Lodge or the Tenants.
  - It's **book only** until decided otherwise. No ARG content about an American civil war goes into real US political conversations, hashtags or communities.
  - Book 1's setting rule (no real present-day towns or institutions) would have to change for Book 2 to name Washington (open question 17).

## The teaser: why epilogue plus ARG drop

- **Impact (in the book):** chapter 30 ends cleanly on *INTERESTING*. The **epilogue**, a few weeks later, delivers two shocks. First, a notice the machine didn't write, addressed *TO THE TENANTS OF THIS HOUSE*, announcing an inspection. Then, for the first time in history, the machine types **I**.
- **Staying power (outside the book):** on launch day, **the notice** is released online as a new in-world artefact, with its date redacted. Readers who finish the book find the story still moving, and that's the start of Book 2's waiting list.
- **Safety:** the drop carries the fiction trail. There's no real date or countdown, and nothing that reads as a real-world prophecy (rules 4–7).
