# Locations & organisations

Every place and body in *Urban Paranoia* is **fictional**. Keep real geography vague: "the city", "the valley", "the north". Never place a Lodge site at a real, identifiable address or building.

---

## Locations

### Harrowgate House
- **What:** a Victorian country house in a fogbound valley, about two and a half hours' drive from the city (matches the 1971 vehicle log).
- **History:**
  - **1958:** leased by the Harlow-Vane Institute as a residential "retreat" for forecasting seminars.
  - **14 Oct 1962:** the Harrowgate Contact. Seven guests stay over; three remain.
  - **1962–71:** the Lodge's meeting place. The teletype runs in the **Listening Room**.
  - **1971:** the teletype goes to the Ashby store and counsel moves to a telephone line in the Listening Room. The lease passes to the Ashby Trust as a "conference centre" that never hosts a conference. The Table still meets here.
  - **1983:** the Exchange cabinet is installed in the Listening Room. Crane, as "H.H. liaison", records sessions here from 1979.
  - **1989:** a fire guts the east wing, including the Listening Room. The Exchange is moved to the Ashby store, and the Table stops meeting at Harrowgate. The wing is never rebuilt.
  - **1994:** the postcode is reassigned during a boundary change. The address "no longer exists", which is why Vesper can't find it.
  - **Today:** a shell. Roofless east wing, sealed west wing, a gravel drive with room for seven cars.
- **Rooms (for flashbacks, the audio drama and the film):**
  - **The Listening Room** (east wing): small, panelled, one chair facing the teletype, a clock. Recording HG-68 was made here.
  - **The Library**: where the Minutes were typed (three carbons).
  - **The Long Room**: the **Table**, thirteen chairs, one always pushed in.
  - **The stable yard**: where Coyle's lorries turned round.
- **Naming caution:** "Harrogate" is a real town, and "Harrowgate" is an old spelling of it. Never connect the house to Harrogate or any real place; the [pre-launch checklist](../campaign/prelaunch-checklist.md) includes a name check.

### The Ashby store
- **What:** a windowless bonded warehouse on an industrial estate outside the city, owned by the Ashby Trust and after 2011 by Larkspire Mutual Re.
- **Contents:** the 1962 teletype, the Exchange cabinet (1983–94), Harlow-Vane's paper archive (since 2009), and copies 1 and 2 of the Harrowgate Minutes in a safe.
- **Role:** the climax of the book (Act 3), where the teletype prints again, unplugged.

### Coyle's sites
- **Head office**: a 1930s brick building near the old goods yard in the city. Emmanuel Okafor worked here from 1979 to 2009, and Vesper's team from 2014 to 2025.
- **The archive storage unit**: by the old rail yard. Keycard access, cameras, all logged through the routing system since 2025 (see [canon decisions D5](canon-decisions.md)).
- **The comms room**: home of the 144 lb server cabinet.

### The Harlow-Vane Institute
- **Offices (1958–2009)**: a Georgian terrace in the city's old quarter. C.S.S. used the basement, with its own teletype line (the 1962 "line connection").
- **Today**: offices converted into flats. The holding website is all that's left in public.

### The city
- Unnamed. A post-industrial Western city with a closed rail yard, delivery-app traffic, rising rents and a mixed neighbourhood around the **Mission Hall**.
- **Vesper's flat**: a second-floor flat on an ordinary street. Graham, of the grey door, lives across the road.
- **The Mission Hall**: Pastor Ama Lindqvist's community hall. It becomes the base for Danny's community network in the book: food bank, job swap, repair café, open to everyone.

### The data centre
- **What:** "**DC-3**", a featureless data centre on a ring road, run by a fictional colocation company, **Coldharbour Data**.
- **Role:** Teo Brandt works nights here and logs the 12×12 grid errors. The Lodge's current contact channel runs through rented racks here.

## Organisations

### The Lodge ("the Table")
See [the-lodge.md](the-lodge.md) and the seat table in [canon-decisions.md](canon-decisions.md).

### The Harlow-Vane Institute for Systems Studies (1958–2009)
- **Public face:** a dull long-range social research institute.
- **Reality:** the Lodge's **thinking arm**. C.S.S. ("Carrying-capacity & Social Stability") turned the Tenants' counsel into models: labour substitution tables, Model 7, the attribution-of-blame paper.
- **Funded** by the Ashby Trust and, from the 1980s, "a long-standing gift from Larkspire Mutual Re" (as its holding site puts it).
- **Hosted international seminars** for promising officials at Harrowgate House from 1960, which is how the seven 1962 guests came to be there.
- **Wound up 2009**, with its assets passing to the Ashby Trust.
- **People:** Hollis Crane (card 0144), Emmanuel Okafor (card 0212, a contractor via Coyle's), the Founders (0001–0003), Marguerite Aldane (0007).

### The Ashby Trust (1955–2011)
- **Public face:** a small charity for "the study of long-term social conditions".
- **Reality:** the Lodge's **property and custody arm**. It held the Harrowgate lease and owned the Ashby store.
- **Dissolved 2011**, with its property passing to Larkspire Mutual Re. This matches Vesper's "dissolved a long time ago" in Post #1, which is 15 years before.

### Larkspire Mutual Re
- **Public face:** a mid-sized reinsurance group, usually just "Larkspire". It insures the insurers against catastrophe.
- **Reality:** the Lodge's **money**. Seat Four (the Ledger) chairs it. The reinsurance business is a dark joke: its job is to price "the long weather".
- **Owns** the Ashby store since 2011.
- **Owns thousands of rented homes** through property funds, including the block Vesper lives in (revealed by the Archivist in Phase 3). See [modern pressures §2](modern-pressures.md).
- **Name history:** first drafted as "Meridian Mutual" and renamed on 25 Oct 2026, because a real US insurer of that name existed (rule 3). Still run the standard name checks before launch.

### Coyle Brothers Haulage
- **Public face:** a family haulier founded in 1912, "invisible, reliable, in the middle of everything".
- **Reality:** the Lodge's **carrier** since 1962. One Coyle grandson was a Steward. The "odd jobs" ran through head office (Emmanuel's calls).
- **Today:** has replaced its routing team with an AI system (Day 22,861).
- **The Coyle family don't know** what the jobs were. The Lodge relies on people who don't ask.

### The fictional global companies (Table holdings)

All fictional. Each is a household name **in the story's world**. **None stands in for one specific real company**: each blends traits so that no real firm or founder is implied. The Table owns quiet minority stakes through Larkspire and the family offices. **The founders and staff of these companies know nothing about the Lodge.**

| Company | What it makes | Founded | Table stake since | Seat |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Pellucid** | Phones, computers, the screens people sleep beside | 1977 | 1979 (pre-launch angel round, via a nominee) | Engine (5) |
| **Tollgate** | The online marketplace everyone buys from, its warehouses, and the cloud servers half the internet rents | 1995 | 1997 | Ledger (4), Signal (3) |
| **Halvard Dynamics** | Electric vehicles, batteries, satellites | 2003 | 2006 | Engine (5), Ground (8) |
| **Nimfold** | The first frontier AI lab | 2015 | 2015 | Engine (5) |
| **Lanternwork** | Nimfold's great rival lab | 2021 | 2021 | Engine (5), Signal (3) |

**Story uses:**
- **Coyle's** routing system runs on a Nimfold model hosted in Tollgate's cloud.
- **DC-3 / Coldharbour Data** rents racks to Lanternwork, and the Lodge's "idle" racks sit among them.
- Vesper's phone is a Pellucid. In Phase 4 she wonders what it has heard.
- **Both AI labs are Table-funded** and racing each other, which is the clearest image of *"LET THEM RACE."*

**Rules:**
- No real company names, logos, products, executives or founding stories.
- Never write a fictional company so close to one real firm that readers would read it as that firm (e.g. no fictional founder modelled on a real tech billionaire).
- Add each name to the [pre-launch name checks](../campaign/prelaunch-checklist.md).

### Coldharbour Data
- **Public face:** a colocation data-centre operator.
- **Reality:** unknowing. The Engine Seat rents racks there through Larkspire Mutual Re.

### The Mission Hall community network (book)
- Not an organisation at first: just Danny, Pastor Ama, neighbours, and later Vesper and Teo.
- **Role in the story's message:** a mixed, local, connected community that the Decoy Doctrine can't split. It's the thing the Tenants call *"Interesting."*
