**Label:** HARROWGATE · 13–14 OCTOBER 1962 (section label: THE DEMOGRAPHER)
**Narration:** close third person. It opens with the Coyle's driver and moves to the British demographer (THREE, never named).
**Dates:** Saturday 13 to Sunday 14 October 1962. Day 0 begins at 02:10 on the Sunday.
**Length:** about 2,950 words. Draft v2.3.

**What happens**
- A young Coyle's driver ("Old Tommy", named later by Len) delivers a teletype to Harrowgate House and is told it will be "needed from tomorrow". He notices seven cars and no staff.
- Seven mid-level officials from seven countries come to a residential seminar run by the Harlow-Vane Institute: the American telephone planner, the German statistician, the demographer, and guests from Tokyo, Rio, Delhi and Le Havre. The host walks away into the fog.
- At 02:10 the unplugged machine starts typing: *SIT. THERE IS TIME.* The counsel is calm and advisory, and never says "I".
- It predicts that *IN EIGHT DAYS THE FIELD WILL BE FRIGHTENED. IT WILL NOT BURN.* (the real October 1962 crisis, unnamed, as backdrop only).
- The three tones (G, E♭, G) sound for the first time.
- Four guests leave, each with a dignified exit. Three stay.
- THREE alone reads *THE ONE WHO COUNTS WILL KEEP THE BOOK* and writes *Day 0.*

**Threads planted**
- The driver's note, found again in ch 3.
- The faceless man at the door: the type of Len's cash man and Cobb.
- No "I" (ch 23, and the epilogue's *I*).
- The four unused place settings (the thirteenth chair).
- The Frenchman's thread (ch 4, ch 26).
- The counters (deep time).
- "It will not burn": the Tenants protect their crop.

**Editor's watch-points:** the guests are deliberately unnamed. The real 1962 crisis is never named and no real people appear.
