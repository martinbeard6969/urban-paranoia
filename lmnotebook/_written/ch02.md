**What happens**
- At a weekend briefing with hotel croissants, Coyle's AI routing system goes live. Nell's team of nine is told nobody is "losing their job today", and over eleven months the team is phased down to one.
- Nell is kept on last to "support the transition". Her letter thanks her for her *contribution to the transition*.
- Her final project is sorting the company archive in a storage unit by the old rail yard.
- At about 4 p.m. on 20 April 2026 she finds a 1971 manifest: one teletype, collected from Harrowgate House for the Ashby Trust, with no weight and no charge, *D. 3062* where the date should be, and a purple stamp: **FOR THE LONG WEATHER**.
- She takes it home and starts a blog as "Vesper", her father's name for his evening crossword hour. It goes live at 03:10 and sits there unread, *"like a lantern in a field."*
