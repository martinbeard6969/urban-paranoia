**What happens**
- A reader emails about a line hidden in the source of Nell's second post: *3062 days out. 22,8xx days in. still counting.* The blog's history shows it was added at 03:12 that night, using her login. She changes every password and, at Danny's insistence, reports it.
- On Saturday a typed letter comes to her home address, using her full name. It has a grey band across the top and is signed with a drawing of a crossed-out kitchen chair. It says *YOU COUNTED BACKWARDS. GOOD. NOW COUNT FORWARDS.* It knows her password was her father's birthday. It warns: *DO NOT BLAME ANYONE YOU CAN SEE FROM YOUR WINDOW. THAT IS WHAT THEY WANT.* And: *WHEN YOU ARE READY THE DOOR IS GREY.*
- Counting forwards 22,861 days reaches Saturday 17 May 2025, the croissants: the day the machine "arrived at her desk".
- Two enclosures, both **144 lb**: the 1971 manifest's consignee's copy (signed "recd. Ashby Tst.") and the 2025 goods-in note for the routing system's server cabinet. Danny: *"Heavy office kit is heavy office kit."* Nell: *"The coincidence might be nothing. The noticing was arranged."*
- At the window she makes herself look at her neighbours without blaming them: Graham and his grey front door, the young mother, the lads, the man with the dog. For one second she suspects Danny, and says "No" out loud.
- Her father, "the man who knew where everything was". She takes down his crossword dictionary, sees his pencil marks, and closes it unread.
