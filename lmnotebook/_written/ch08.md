**Label:** HOLLIS CRANE · HARROWGATE, 1979
**Narration:** close third, opening outside him; in chapter 1's tone.
**Dates:** one night, autumn 1979.
**Length:** about 1,900 words. Draft v1.0 (30 Oct 2026).

**What happens**
- A taxi driver takes Crane, 34, for a piano tuner. The German: "You are the new arithmetic."
- The Listening Room: tape recorder, six pencils, the cream telephone. Mother Quill, knitting, holds the receiver out. Crosstalk with the rhythm of speech: *A FIELD CAN BE THINNED BEFORE THE DROUGHT OR AFTER IT… COUNT THE DIFFERENCE.*
- Overnight he draws two columns. *After:* nine figures. *Before:* nobody dies; people just say "not this year". He weeps with happiness. The rows are labelled *persons*.
- The demographer: "It's very good." The Canadian's car arrives. She gives Crane a ruled notebook.

**Ends:** he goes upstairs humming *We plough the fields*, and sings the descant.
