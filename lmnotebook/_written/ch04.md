**Label:** THE DEMOGRAPHER · DAY 3,061 · HARROWGATE, 2 MARCH 1971 (section label: THE ACTUARY · LE HAVRE, THE SAME NIGHT)
**Narration:** interlude, close third person. First the demographer (THREE), then the French actuary who left in 1962.
**Dates:** the night of Tuesday 2 – Wednesday 3 March 1971 (Day 3,061; the counsel arrives at 02:10 on Day 3,062). Coyle's collects the machine in the morning.
**Length:** about 2,000 words. Draft v3.3 (redrafted 28 Oct 2026 as one continuous scene; polished 29 Oct; fixes v1.6, 30 Oct).

**What happens**
- The demographer arrives by taxi in a cashmere coat and made shoes, pays without looking at the meter, then goes back to count the change. The American's driver waits all night; the German's grey saloon has a measured dent. There are still no staff: the twelve carry their own plates.
- Dinner in the Long Room. The Rotterdam shipping manager reports tonnage "up a third. Again." The American toasts "the boxes". *They had been nobodies.* She asks how many men a dock gang was: forty, now "a crane. And the man who drives the crane." She writes *39* in her notebook margin and can't find its column.
- The thirteenth chair is always pushed in: on the first night the Rotterdam man reached for it and the German said *no*.
- The telephone in the hall rings once. Nobody mentions it, or the German's hair, which went white the winter he tried to disprove the line.
- The woman from New Zealand (the future Mother Quill), silent all evening, says: *"It's going, isn't it… At home we'd say the glass was falling."*
- At midnight the three go to the Listening Room. The German weighed the machine years ago on a grain scale: **144 lb**. At the stroke of twelve she writes *Day 3,062*.
- At 02:10: *THE LINE IS OPEN.* / *THE MACHINE IS NOT NEEDED HERE. IT WILL BE KEPT.* / *KEPT.* Coyle's will come in the morning, with no weight on the driver's sheet and the purple stamp. Asked what it will wait for: *NOT FAR. ONLY ELSEWHERE.* Then the three notes (recording HG-71). She thinks it could mean them: not needed, and kept.
- In Le Havre the same night, the actuary looks out at the container berth and the dockers' closed café ("Nobody had asked him to price the men"), plays the three tones on his mother's piano, feels a presence, and closes the lid for good. Twenty-six years later his son sells the piano, badly out of tune.
