**What happens**
- **Fast open:** with nine days of notice left, Nell races the 1971 vehicle logs, standing at the shelf, and finds Wednesday 3 March 1971: *H.H. — per instr.* The red camera light is on when she goes in, and **off when she comes out**.
- Four hundred readers. On the Friday a reader with an owl avatar suggests "D. 3062" is a count of days. Nell's sum gives **Day 0 = 14 October 1962**, "a week before the world held its breath".
- She walks past the old head office and nearly phones her father. She has never deleted his number.
- On Monday she rings two retired drivers. One laughs. Len goes quiet, says he's seen it "on the odd job", asks after her mum, and agrees to meet.
- **The mistake:** that night, pleased with herself, she adds the drivers paragraph to Post #1: *"(I'm reading something into that.)"* No name, no place. Forty-one readers by morning. She doesn't delete it, because "a deletion is the loudest thing you can do on the internet."
- HR's return-of-property list includes her laptop, phone, lanyard and permit, but not the keycard. Nobody collects it.
- **The café:** Len has seen the paragraph ("Is this you, Len?"). *"How many drivers d'you think went quiet, love? There's about six of us left."* Nell fears for him, then for herself: her settlement's clause about company records, and a keycard that logged every visit. *"Leave it. Taking it off's worse."*
- Len's memories: the off-book runs, the polite cash man with a face that "didn't stick", a wardrobe-sized machine that "hummed with the power off".
- **An unnamed man sits at the next table** (Cobb, sighting 1). Len stops talking until he leaves. *"Who else did you tell?" "Nobody. I didn't say where."* Neither can describe him. Later her paragraph has been reposted on its own by a nameless account with no other posts.
- Len tells **Old Tommy's** 1962 story: *needed from tomorrow*, written on the docket "so it'd be somewhere".
- Terry asked who the cash man was and lost the good runs. *"You asked on the internet, love."* Len is welcoming her, not warning her.
